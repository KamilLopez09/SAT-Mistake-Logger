# PrepPros Math → Question-Bank Map (Level 3/4 focus, 750–800)

**Goal.** Make the PrepPros practice you do *now* pay off in the final week. You are working the PrepPros
book front-to-back (Level 2–4 problems in every chapter). This document ties each chapter's **Level 3/4
(hard) practice** to the matching **hard questions in the College Board Math Question Bank** (`Copy of Math
Qs.pdf`), so that in the last week before the **Aug 22** SAT you can review by skill instead of hunting.

> Reminder of your plan: **Math = prep book now** (tons of practice per chapter) → **final week: review
> mistakes + question bank only.** **RW = question bank in conjunction now** (few practice problems).
> This map is the bridge for the math half.

---

## How the difficulty levels line up

PrepPros grades every concept/problem Level 1–4; the Question Bank tags every question Easy/Medium/Hard.
They line up approximately like this:

| PrepPros level | Target score | ≈ Question-bank difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 – Core Fundamentals | < 550 | Easy |
| Level 2 – Additional Fundamentals | 550–650 | Easy / Medium |
| **Level 3 – Advanced Topics** | 650–720 | **Medium / Hard** |
| **Level 4 – Expert Level Topics** | **730–800** | **Hard (very hard)** |

Because you're aiming for **750–800 (Level 4)**, PrepPros itself says to *"understand EVERYTHING in this
book … work through the Level 2–4 questions in ALL chapters."* So **every chapter is "recommended,"** and
the tables below cover all 36. For each chapter, the **Level 4 review target = the Hard bank questions**
listed; the **Level 3 review target = the Medium ones.**

**This bank export contains 120 questions: 65 Hard, 35 Medium, 20 Easy.** It is weighted toward
Geometry & Trigonometry (51) and Advanced Math (27), then Algebra (23) and Problem-Solving & Data
Analysis (19) — so the hard practice is densest exactly where the adaptive Module 2 piles on.

Page numbers (e.g. `p74`) are the question's page in `Copy of Math Qs.pdf`. Full Question IDs are in the
appendix at the bottom.

## Coverage notes / gaps to know

- **No Hard questions in this export for:** *Algebra · Linear inequalities* (Ch 8) and *PSDA · Two-variable
  data / scatterplots* (Ch 26). Only Medium exist here — get extra hard reps for these from Bluebook's
  full Question Bank if they're a weak spot.
- **`Evaluating statistical claims (observational studies & experiments)`** (part of Ch 22) has **no
  questions at all** in this export. Practice it in the live Question Bank.
- **Densest hard practice:** Geometry & Trig — *Area and volume* (8 Hard), *Circles* (8 Hard, feeds Ch 27 &
  33), *Right triangles & trig* (7 Hard, feeds Ch 15 & 20) — and Advanced Math — *Nonlinear functions*
  (7 Hard, feeds Ch 10/11/13/19/28/29/35/36) and *Nonlinear equations* (6 Hard). Prioritize these; they're
  both high-frequency and high-difficulty.
- Several PrepPros chapters share one bank skill (e.g. Ch 27 & 33 → *Circles*; Ch 12, 31, 32 → *Systems*).
  Don't re-drill the same bank question twice — the reverse index dedupes this for you.

## Suggested workflow (ties into the Mistake Logger)

1. **Now → Aug 15ish:** work PrepPros front-to-back, all Level 2–4 problems per chapter. Log every miss in
   the Mistake Logger tagged with the **bank skill** from Table A (not the chapter name) so it lines up with
   step 3.
2. **After finishing the book:** you already have a mistake log keyed to bank skills.
3. **Final week (Aug 15–22):** for each skill where you missed the most (pivot the log by skill), open the
   Question Bank and do the **Hard** pages listed for that skill in Table B. This is pure hard-question
   review, exactly matching what you practiced.
4. Re-drill any you miss again in the last 2–3 days (PrepPros' own "repeat wrong questions" advice).
