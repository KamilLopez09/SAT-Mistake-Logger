# Brown University movie clubs and classic-film outings

Notes from a search of Brown’s student-organization directory, campus event listings, and recent news. Checked October 2026.

Brown does not have a recognized student club whose main activity is outdoor classic-film nights or regular trips to a repertory theatre. The closest matches are one graduate film club, a few student groups that screen movies indoors, and department or university programs that show older films.

## Closest student organization

**Film Club, Brown University** is a Graduate Student Council–recognized group.

- Purpose: watch student-chosen films, with an emphasis on cinema that is not a new release, plus discussion and occasional guest speakers.
- Meetings: described as biweekly.
- Contact: filmclub@brown.edu
- The public listing does not say the screenings are outdoors or at a commercial theatre.
- Profile: https://studentactivities.brown.edu/organizations/film-club-brown-university

An older group, the **Brown University Film Forum**, used to help with classic and restored-film programs. Students on Reddit in August 2024 described it as inactive since about 2020, and it does not appear in the current student-organization directory.

## Student groups that go to a theatre

These groups use theatres. They are not classic-film clubs.

### Ivy Film Festival

Large student-run festival staffed by Brown and RISD students. Festival week is mostly new student films, advance screenings, and talks.

- Venues include Martinos Auditorium in the Granoff Center and the Avon Cinema on Thayer Street.
- The 2026 schedule (April 13–19) included a screening at the Avon.
- Site: https://www.ivyfilmfestival.org/

### Brown Motion Pictures

Student-run production club, founded in 1987. Members produce short films each semester. End-of-semester premieres of those student films are held at the Avon Cinema, rather than a classics program.

- Profile: https://studentactivities.brown.edu/organizations/motion-pictures-brown-university
- Site: https://brownmotionpictures.org/

### Brown Original Series

Produces episodic student series and premieres them at the Avon Cinema (for example, *Shopfront* and *Pas de Deux* in March 2025).

- Profile: https://studentactivities.brown.edu/organizations/original-series-brown

### Brown Film Magazine

Writing community for film and television (reviews, interviews, a print issue). Not a screening club.

- Profile: https://studentactivities.brown.edu/organizations/film-magazine-brown

Other recognized film groups (Animation Team, Screenwriting Collective, Studio ’64) make or write work. They do not run classic-film outings.

## Where classics actually get shown

These are campus programs with student involvement, not recognized outing clubs.

### Magic Lantern Cinema

Long-running series tied to the Department of Modern Culture and Media and the Malcolm S. Forbes Center for Culture and Media Studies. Students, artists, and local programmers have run it. Screenings are indoors, often on 35mm in Martinos Auditorium.

Recent titles:

- *Videodrome* (1983), January 2024
- *Nosferatu the Vampyre* (1979), March 2024
- *The Bird with the Crystal Plumage* (1970), April 22, 2025

### Il Cinema Ritrovato on Tour

Run by the Department of Italian Studies with the Cineteca of Bologna. Graduate students help curate and staff it. Restored classics play in Martinos Auditorium. The twelfth edition was March 12–15, 2025 (themes included migration, political repression, and homages to Marcello Mastroianni and Donald Sutherland). Free and open to the public.

- Site: http://cineritrovatobrown.weebly.com/

### Rigorously Curated

Brown Arts Institute semester series, guest-curated by faculty, students, and visitors. Indoor auditorium screenings.

- Fall 2025 in Martinos Auditorium included *Suspiria* (1977), plus *Cats*, *Black Swan*, *Ex Machina*, *Hedwig & the Angry Inch*, *Twilight*, *Annihilation*, and *Arrival*.
- Spring 2026 included *The Day After Tomorrow* (March 5, Martinos).
- Fall 2026 (November 10–12) moves to the Lindemann Performing Arts Center and includes *Sleep Dealer* (2008), *The Mitchells vs. the Machines* (2021), and *Ghost in the Shell* (1995).
- Page: https://arts.brown.edu/programs/rigorously-curated

### Film-Thinking

Cogut Institute series convened by English professor Timothy Bewes. Each event is a curated screening plus a conversation. Faculty program, not a student club.

- Page: https://humanities.brown.edu/events/film-thinking

Cultural student groups sometimes host one-off indoor series. In February 2022, SWANA Heritage Series, the Iranian Students Association, the Armenian Society, and the Arab Society ran a month of screenings at the Salomon Center, including the 1996 Iranian film *Gabbeh*.

## Everyday theatre option

Students mostly go on their own to the **Avon Cinema** on Thayer Street, an art-house theatre that mixes new independent films with special events. Brown Motion Pictures, Brown Original Series, and the Ivy Film Festival all use it. A non-Brown **Providence Movie Club** also organizes theatre meetups; students have pointed to it when no campus watching club was active.
