# RW Question Classification — Literal Comprehension vs. Reasonable Inferences

A divided list of the questions in **`Copy of RW questions.pdf`** that fall under the two
*Information and Ideas* skills below. Each question was matched two ways:

1. **Skill / Domain tag** printed on the question (metadata row), and
2. **Question-stem phrasing** — compared against the sample phrasings in the Chapter 5 and
   Chapter 6 reference screenshots, *ignoring the passage content* each stem is paired with.

The source PDF contains **150 questions** (one per page). Of these, **13** are tagged
*Central Ideas and Details* and **16** are tagged *Inferences*. Page numbers below refer to the
PDF page (= question number).

## Chapter 5 — Literal Comprehension

- **Domain:** Information and Ideas
- **Skill:** Central Ideas and Details (extracting explicit claims, main ideas, or direct facts from the passage)
- **Reference phrasing (screenshot):** *"According to the text, what is true about X?"*, *"According to the text, why…?"*, *"Based on the text, how does X respond?"* — i.e. what the passage **states or indicates**.

## Chapter 6 — Reasonable Inferences

- **Domain:** Information and Ideas
- **Skill:** Inferences (selecting the choice that most logically completes the text based only on the provided premises)
- **Reference phrasing (screenshot):** *"It can be most reasonably inferred…"*, *"The author suggests that…"*, *"…would most likely agree with which statement…"*, *"An unstated assumption in the passage is that…"* — i.e. what the passage **suggests or implies**.

Every question tagged *Inferences* in this PDF uses the digital-SAT completion stem
**"Which choice most logically completes the text?"** (the passage ends in a blank `______`).
This is the canonical form of the skill defined above — the reader must infer the conclusion
that follows *only* from the given premises — so all 16 match the Chapter 6 question family.
